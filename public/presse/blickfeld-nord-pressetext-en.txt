BLICKFELD NORD – Press text

Party band with three voices and plenty of atmosphere

What started in 2019 as a duo of David Bröker and Matthias Wigger is now a real power trio: since October 2025, Jan-Frederic “Fred” Overbeck on bass has completed the line-up. Ever since, it’s been three voices, one goal – party.

Blickfeld Nord plays music that gets your feet moving and invites everyone to sing along – a varied repertoire of pop, rock, German Schlager, dance and disco. Whether it’s a Christmas market, a town festival or a private party: when Blickfeld Nord is on stage, nobody stays seated for long.

Line-up:
– David Bröker: Vocals & guitar
– Matthias Wigger: Drums & vocals
– Jan-Frederic Overbeck: Bass & vocals

For organisers:
– Occasions: Festivals, private parties and pubs
– Playing time: 3 to 4 hours – in sets of 1 hour
– Breaks: As agreed or spontaneously
– Equipment: We bring our own PA and lights
